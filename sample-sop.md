## 6. Process Overview

1. Take a queue number
2. Verify documents
3. Enter data into the system
4. Inform the applicant

---

## 7. Procedure Steps

### Step 1: Verify documents

Check that the ID and the application form match.

### Step 2: Determine household registration

- Registered elsewhere than residence
  - Household registered at actual place of residence
    - The local sub-district office accepts the application
  - All or most household registrations at one address
- Collective household

### Step 3: Inform the applicant

"Your documents have been received. You will get a reply within X business days."

---

## 8. Exception Handling

| Exception | How to Handle |
|---|---|
| Incomplete documents | Inform the applicant of all missing items at once; issue a correction notice |
| Incorrect information | Return the application and explain why |
